# Nitrone drawing rule

COFdraw constructs the local nitrone motif `Ar–CH=[N+]([O−])–Ar` from an aromatic aldehyde and a neutral arylhydroxylamine `Ar–NH–OH`. This motif follows Scheme 1 of Kurandina et al., *A Porous Crystalline Nitrone-Linked Covalent Organic Framework*, DOI [10.1002/anie.202307674](https://doi.org/10.1002/anie.202307674). The [author-hosted primary paper](https://yaghi.berkeley.edu/pdfPublications/23NitroneCOF.pdf) provides the precursor and product structures.

The transform removes the aldehyde oxygen, retains the hydroxylamine N–O and aryl–N bonds, removes the reactive N–H/O–H hydrogens from the product patch, and assigns formal charges N +1 and O −1. It connects the aldehyde carbon to that nitrogen with a double bond. New C=N stereochemistry remains unspecified; a drawn bond orientation does not assign E/Z. Ordinary primary amines are not accepted as hydroxylamines. O-protected, N-disubstituted, protonated or charged starting groups, alkyl hydroxylamines and isotope-labelled reactive hydrogen require a separate explicit recipe. Nitrogen and oxygen isotope labels are retained.

The runtime linkage ID is `nitrone`, with ordered roles `aryl_aldehyde` and `aryl_hydroxylamine`. Each monomer needs at least two supported sites for a patch. Auto mode can select this unique compatible chemistry and maps constructor roles without changing the user's visible A/B choices. A primary amine partner cannot trigger this rule. Description plans validate the same reaction and functional-site counts before becoming ready.

The output remains a finite designed chemical patch with continuation ports. A chosen square, hexagonal or custom template does not identify the structure as COF-115, establish its reported kgm topology, predict crystallization, or generate an optimized periodic crystal. Unused sites are outgoing product-continuation ports, not residual precursor groups. The paper's separate photo-rearrangement is not implemented by this construction rule.

Regression checks cover the source-depicted tetratopic hydroxylamine, a two-site model, negative functional-group controls, charge and N–O preservation, aldehyde oxygen removal, isotope retention, connected patches, actual API preparation/rendering, and MOL/SDF/CDXML chemical export. These checks establish the implemented graph transformation; they do not validate a synthetic procedure or material properties.
