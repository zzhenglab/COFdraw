The desktop app loads `framework-drawing/catalog/current.json`. The readable current file is backed by hash-addressed catalog history and `current.audit.json`; old source evidence and catalogs remain unchanged. Production and portable copies bind different recipe hashes because the portable recipes have private paths removed. Both copies verify their own registry, four recipe identities, exact precursor graphs and full SMILES-based COFex strings.

The catalog contains 41 source-reviewed monomers and 12 starting-pair presets. `connection_count` and `reactive_groups` describe reactive sites. `node_symmetry` is separate and remains unassigned where it has not been independently established. A C2 symmetry label must never be interpreted as two reactive sites. One primary example explicitly uses four-connected D2h and two-connected C2 building blocks. [Primary source](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2023/cc/d3cc04502a)

The topology browser contains 25 entries covering nets, pore shapes and design strategies. Only seven explicit drawing template IDs are enabled: `hcb_3_2`, `hcb_3_3`, `sql_4_2`, `sql_4_4`, `hxl_6_2`, `chain_2_2` and `alternating_hexagon`. The last two assign no net code. Other entries, including 3D nets, multicomponent and double-stage strategies, are reference information. The user-supplied review figures have no bibliographic caption in this catalog; their labels retain that evidence gap. The supplied spelling `ljh` remains unverified rather than being silently normalized.

Site count does not determine geometry, net or synthetic feasibility. A study obtained different pore arrangements from monomers with the same symmetries by changing substituents. [Primary source](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlelanding/2017/sc/c6sc05673c) Triangular/tetrahedral components can form different 3D nets, while conformation can enable another outcome for three- and four-connected components. [ctn/bor research](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17431178/), [fjh research](https://yaghi.berkeley.edu/pdfPublications/20COF3D.pdf)

The six-site triptycenes in the monomer library are nonplanar. Selecting `hxl_6_2` produces a chosen finite projection, not evidence for an hxl material. Tetrahedral four-site nodes are rejected for named planar templates. Source COFex identity remains separate from design notation; design `.cat0` is an explicit assumption, and the full precursor notation is not a complete periodic framework SMILES.

`scripts/update_cofdraw_current_catalog.py` publishes both contexts and checks all 12 preset/template counts. `tests/test_cofdraw_expanded_chemistry.py` adds 22 fast regression cases covering six linkage states, local bond preservation, carbonyl/N–N/N–H counts, reactive-carbon hydrogen counts, hydrazide specificity, exact hydrazine forms, template ports and identity invalidation after incidence edits. These tests avoid global macrocycle canonicalization and make no crystallographic accuracy claim.
