# Bounded linkage taxonomy review

Independent review on 17 November 2026 of the v3 catalog, its builder, `cofdraw_chemistry.library_linkages`, the local research catalog and selected primary literature. This is a taxonomy/source check, not a new full-paper annotation batch, a renderer implementation or evidence that arbitrary designs can be synthesized.

The runtime exposes six scoped transforms. It correctly keeps other catalog entries disabled, validates compatible functional roles, and requires a separate attachment template. The v3 catalog contains 24 entries, including three historical aliases: hydrazone/acylhydrazone, paper-labelled hydrazine-link/azine, and imine-to-amine/reduced-imine secondary amine. It should not be described as 24 distinct linkage chemistries. `aminal` and `ketazine` appear in `reference_only_compatibility`; that field alone does not put them in the searchable linkage list produced from `linkage_catalog`.

| Family | Chemical handles and local state | Scope and primary source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Imine | Aromatic aldehyde + primary arylamine; C=N, with aldehyde O removed in condensation | Retain reactive atom identity; neither E/Z nor topology follows from site count. [COF-300, Uribe-Romo et al. 2009](https://yaghi.berkeley.edu/pdfPublications/09Crystalline.pdf), DOI 10.1021/ja8096256. |
| Beta-ketoenamine | Tp + primary arylamine; coupled ring carbonyl/exocyclic C=C/NH state | Current transform requires exact unsubstituted Tp. Other hydroxy-aldehydes need their own rule. [Primary Tp/diamine study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10751766/) explicitly distinguishes keto/enol states and validates the product spectroscopically. |
| Acylhydrazone | Aldehyde + terminal H2N–NH–C(O); product C=N–NH–C(O) | Preserve the original acyl carbonyl and the N–N bond. It is not an azine. [COF-42/43, Uribe-Romo et al. 2011](https://yaghi.berkeley.edu/pdfPublications/11-crystallinecof.pdf), DOI 10.1021/ja204728y. |
| Tp keto-enamine with retained acylhydrazide | Tp + terminal acylhydrazide; keto Tp with exocyclic CH–NH–NH–C(O) chain | The internal key `keto_hydrazide` is not a universal family-level tautomer rule. TpODH and TFBODH explicitly differ in drawn bond/NH state despite broad use of “hydrazone” in the paper. [Primary TpODH/TFBODH article](https://doi.org/10.1021/acsami.8b18502), main Fig.1 and pp.2–3; local source and connected-graph audits are bound under `root-r698`. |
| Azine | Two aldehydes + neutral hydrazine; C=N–N=C | A hydrazine hydrate/salt and an acylhydrazide are different inputs. The cited paper's “hydrazine-linked” wording describes the azine bond pattern; its later partial hydrazide oxidation is a separate state. [Nguyen et al. 2022](https://yaghi.berkeley.edu/pdfPublications/22COFWater.pdf), DOI 10.1021/acscentsci.2c00398. |
| Reduced-imine secondary amine | An imine-derived CH2–NH product state | Preserve parent/product distinction and residual conversion. It is not generic aldehyde/amine condensation. [Grunenberg et al. 2021](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7953377/), DOI 10.1021/jacs.0c12249, demonstrates condition-dependent reduction and mixed-linkage states. |
| Boroxine, reference only | Three boronic-acid sites condense into one B3O3 cycle | Three-site ring formation cannot be represented by an ordinary two-port join. [Côté et al. 2005](https://yaghi.berkeley.edu/pdfPublications/cote_yaghi.pdf), DOI 10.1126/science.1120411, Fig.1. |
| Boronate ester, reference only | Boronic acid + vicinal diol; retain both diol oxygens in cyclic B–O–C connectivity | Distinct from boroxine and charged borate motifs; no generic single-bond connector. [Côté et al. 2005](https://yaghi.berkeley.edu/pdfPublications/cote_yaghi.pdf), Fig.1 COF-5 chemistry. |
